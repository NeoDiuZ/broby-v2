FAQ

The stuff vets actually ask us.

This is the question we hear most, and it's the right one. Broby only writes from what's said in the room. It never invents findings or numbers. Every note lands as a draft, your name off it, until you read it, tweak anything, and sign. Nothing hits the record without you. It's a fast first draft from someone who was listening, not a replacement for your judgement.
Yes, that was the whole point. Broby lives in a Chrome sidebar next to the tools you already use. If someone can open a browser tab, they can use it. No manual, no training video. Most vets are comfortable inside their first consult.
Yes. It runs as a sidebar inside ezyVet, picks up the patient you already have open, and drops the finished SOAP note straight into the exam notes. No copy-paste, no tab-juggling. (ezyVet is the system we support today.)
Good instinct to ask. A quick heads-up is best practice, and most vets just fold it into how they already explain things ("I use a tool that helps me write up the visit"). Broby records to write the note, then raw audio is deleted on a rolling schedule. You're getting a note, not building a library of recordings.
That's exactly what we built it for. A consult can open in English, slide into Singlish, then switch to Cantonese with the owner, and Broby follows the whole thing. Most "AI scribes" are really English scribes with a regional label. Broby is built for how clinics here actually talk.
It's built around veterinary language. Species, breeds, drug names, dosages, and the shorthand you actually write (it knows "BCS 6/9" is a body condition score, not a typo). If it's ever unsure, it flags instead of guessing.
You won't lose your work. Broby saves as you record, so a crash, dead battery, closed tab or dropped connection doesn't wipe the session. Open Chrome again and it's right where you left off.
Your clinic owns its data, full stop. Audio and notes are encrypted, raw recordings auto-delete on a rolling schedule, and you can export or delete everything whenever you want. We're built to Singapore and Malaysia PDPA standards and never sell identifiable data. The exact retention periods are in our Privacy Policy.
